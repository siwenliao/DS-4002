### Data File
See file Hotdog Survey Responses.csv

### Data Collection Objective

The data was collected through a survey sent out by members in the Spring 2026 DS-4002 Section-001 class to determine if UVA students believe that a hotdog is a sandwich. 

### Data Dictionary
| Column| Description| Potential Reponses|                   
|-------|------------|-------------------|
| Timestamp | Indicates the month, day, and year and hour, minute, and second that the responder completed the survey |month/day/year hour:minute:second|
| Year at UVA | Indicates what UVA year the responder is considered as, or other/not affiliated if they are currently not a student at UVA |1st Year, 2nd Year, 3rd Year, 4th Year, Graduate Student, Other/Not Affiliated|
| What is your Gender Identity?| Indicates what gender the responder identifies as or if they prefer not to share| Man, Woman, Nonbinary, Prefer not to say|
| What Schools are you a Part of?| Indicates what school at UVA the student is enrolled under or affiliated with| Data Science, Arts and Sciences, Engineering, Law, McIntire School of Commerce, Architecture, Darden School of Busines, Batten School of Public Policy, Education|
| Is a Hotdog a Sandwich?| Indicates if responder believes if a hotdog is a sandwich |Yes, No|
| How strongly do you feel about your previous answer?| Indicates how strongly the responder feels about the answer to "Is a Hotdog a Sandwich?" |1, 2, 3, 4, 5|
| How frequently do you eat hot dogs? | Indicates how frequently the responder eats hot dogs | Never, Occasionally (ex. once every few months), Rarely (ex. once a year), Frequently (ex. multiple times a month)|
